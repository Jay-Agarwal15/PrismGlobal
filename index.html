Prism Global: Manufacturing Services
from Prototyping to Production

With our own factory in Shenzhen and a strong network of vetted manufacturers across China, we offer a broad range of manufacturing capabilities from CNC machining to injection molding, sheet metal, and 3D printing. Whether you need fast-turn prototypes or low-to-mid volume production, we're your trusted partner for complex, custom parts delivered with speed and precision.

CNC Machining

CNC milling, turning & precision machining for prototypes and end-use parts. ±0.001" tolerance, 0.001" thin walls, 1-day lead times.

Injection Molding

Get plastic injection molding, overmolding & insert molding with fast & production tooling. No MOQ. 71 samples in 2 weeks. Scale to 1M units.

Sheet Metal Fabrication

Expert laser cutting, bending, welding & stamping. Fast, precise sheet metal prototypes to production. 3-day lead time, 0.008 in. thin walls.

3D Printing

SLA, MJF, MF, SLS & SLM for functional prototypes & low-volume 3D printing. ±0.008 in. precision, 1-day lead time.

Extrusion

Offer high-quality, low-cost metal extrusions with a variety of post-machining options. Rapid & precise delivery.

Die Casting

High-volume precision die casting: Aluminum/Zinc/Magnesium/Copper. 0.001" walls, 1000+ batches.

Vacuum Casting

Rapid soft-tooled plastic prototypes at ±0.002" precision. 15 & up. Cost-effective small-batch alternative to injection molding. Ideal for test iterations.

Wire EDM

Precision sinker & wire EDM. Sharp inside corners & thin walls. Burn-free micromachining with 0.001" accuracy. Best ideal for tooling-free complex geometries.
